PRIORITIZED CHANGES:
Abandon the flat vector style entirely in favor of a photographic or painterly approach that emphasizes texture and decay. The central image should be a photograph of a coastal road or a dilapidated structure that is partially obscured by fog, grain, or double-exposure effects to visualize the 'disappearing' theme. The 'houses' should not be icons, but architectural details that fade into the negative space. The color palette should shift from flat grey to a moody, desaturated teal or bruised purple to evoke the 'winter' setting and melancholy. The typography should be integrated more aggressively—perhaps allowing the title to fade in and out or be partially obscured by the atmospheric elements of the image. The tagline 'Some places disappear twice' should be treated as a ghostly echo in the composition.
PROTECT:
The core concept of the road leading to a vanishing point is structurally sound and effectively communicates the idea of a journey or a limit. The hierarchy of the text is currently legible and should be maintained, even if the styling changes.
TRADE-OFF:
We are trading the current 'cleanliness' and digital precision for 'grain' and ambiguity. The design will become less 'tidy' and more textured/organic to achieve the requested cinematic and emotional depth.
