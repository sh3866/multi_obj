PRIORITIZED CHANGES:
Abandon the geometric/low-poly aesthetic entirely. Replace the central figure with a high-contrast, expressive visual that suggests organic movement—such as a long-exposure photograph of a dancer, a charcoal sketch, or a fragmented silhouette that implies a body in collapse or flight. The visual should feel tactile and physical, not digital. Re-integrate the typography so it interacts with the movement of the figure (e.g., the text could be warped by the 'wind' of the movement or anchored by the 'weight' of the body). Use a more atmospheric color palette or texture (grain, shadow, depth) to replace the flat black void, creating a sense of space and volume.
PROTECT:
The conceptual layering of the text and the figure is a strong starting point. The idea of the body intersecting with the title creates a visual metaphor for the performance's theme. The stark contrast of the current layout is effective for legibility and should be maintained, even if the textures change.
TRADE-OFF:
Prioritize emotional resonance and organic texture over geometric precision. The revision sacrifices the 'clean' digital look in favor of a grittier, more ambiguous, and physically grounded aesthetic that better suits the 'contemporary dance' genre.
