PRIORITIZED CHANGES:
Abandon the centered, symmetrical layout in favor of an asymmetric composition that creates visual tension. Replace the generic silhouette with a more expressive visual metaphor—such as a fragmented body, a high-contrast shadow, or a figure caught in a moment of collapse or suspension—to better represent the 'weight' of the title. Re-integrate the typography: allow the title to interact with the figure (e.g., the figure pushing against the text, or the text distorting the figure) rather than simply sitting on top. Use the negative space to suggest the 'air' mentioned in the title, perhaps by varying the density of the black background or using texture to imply atmosphere. The type hierarchy should be more dynamic, perhaps using the weight of the font to literally demonstrate the concept of 'weight' versus 'air.'
PROTECT:
The high-contrast black and white palette is the strongest element. It provides a sophisticated, dramatic canvas that is appropriate for a contemporary performance and allows for strong visual impact. The choice of a clean, modern sans-serif typeface is also a solid foundation that should be retained, though its application needs to be more expressive.
TRADE-OFF:
The revision prioritizes conceptual clarity and emotional resonance over the current 'clean' and 'safe' aesthetic. This means moving away from the perfectly legible, centered hierarchy toward a layout that may be more visually complex or abstract, accepting that the viewer must engage more actively with the image to understand the relationship between the body and the text.
