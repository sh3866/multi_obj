Transform the neon line into a visual metaphor for a fire front—perhaps a jagged, glowing ember line or a heat-haze distortion that threatens to consume the mountain. Move the headline 'ONE SPARK IS ENOUGH' to the top center or integrate it more dynamically with the negative space. PROTECT:

TRADE-OFF:
Prioritize emotional realism over graphic stylization. The design must sacrifice the 'clean' vector aesthetic to gain the visceral impact of a real wildfire threat, ensuring the viewer feels the danger rather than just seeing a graphic representation of it.
