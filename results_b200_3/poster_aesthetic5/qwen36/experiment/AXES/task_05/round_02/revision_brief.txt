Align the tagline and credits to the same margin (ragged edge) to establish a strong vertical rhythm that contrasts with the horizontal spread of the road. This forces the viewer to scan the composition rather than just looking at the center. PROTECT: The atmospheric gradient and the geometric simplicity of the road and house silhouettes are strong. The 'fading' effect of the houses effectively communicates the theme of memory and disappearance and must be preserved.

Apply a vertical gradient mask to the text so the letters fade from opaque at the bottom to transparent at the top, mimicking the visual language of the fading houses. Increase the scale of the headline to dominate the upper third, allowing the text to act as a structural element of the landscape. PROTECT: The atmospheric color palette and the geometric perspective of the road leading into the fog are strong visual anchors that establish the mood effectively.
