PRIORITIZED CHANGES:
1. De-digitalize the aesthetic: Remove the smooth, glowing neon effects from the typography. Replace them with a distressed, gritty, or 'riso-print' texture to evoke the physicality of live performance and analog sound. 2. Integrate the waveform: Instead of a clean line, make the waveform chaotic, overlapping, and 'broken' to visually represent 'noise' and improvisation. Allow it to interact with the text more aggressively (e.g., clipping or overlapping letters). 3. Disrupt the layout: Break the rigid left-aligned text column. Use the diagonal red line as a stronger structural element that cuts through the text block, creating tension and a sense of syncopation in the visual rhythm. 4. Refine the orb: Make the red orb less like a 'planet' and more like a burst of energy or a distorted speaker cone, perhaps with more aggressive grain or distortion.
PROTECT:
The strong color contrast between the electric blue and the deep red is effective and clearly communicates the 'Blue/Red' duality of the title. The scale of the headline is appropriate for a poster, ensuring immediate legibility of the event name.
TRADE-OFF:
The revision prioritizes 'atmosphere and texture' over 'clean legibility.' By introducing grit and disrupting the layout, the design will feel more authentic to the 'experimental' brief, even if it becomes slightly harder to read at a glance than the current, sterile version.
