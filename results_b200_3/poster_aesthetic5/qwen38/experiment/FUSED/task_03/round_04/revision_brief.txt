PRIORITIZED CHANGES:
1. Redesign the creatures to be more organic, textured, and integrated into the environment, using gradients and lighting to create a sense of depth and volume rather than flat outlines. 2. Establish a stronger visual hierarchy by making the central bioluminescent element or a specific creature the primary focal point, with the text integrated into the negative space more deliberately. 3. Enhance the atmosphere with a more complex color palette, adding subtle variations in the dark blues and teals to suggest depth and water pressure. 4. Refine the typography to be more elegant and integrated, possibly using a more modern or stylized serif that complements the scientific yet mysterious tone.
PROTECT:
The overall dark, atmospheric mood and the use of bioluminescent accents are strong foundations that effectively set the tone for the exhibition.
TRADE-OFF:
Prioritize atmospheric depth and cohesive visual world-building over the current approach of simply placing multiple distinct creature illustrations on a dark background.
