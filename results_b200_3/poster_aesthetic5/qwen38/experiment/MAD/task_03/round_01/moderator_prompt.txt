- Typography & Message Integration: Defend typography as an expressive visual material, not merely readable text. - Color, Atmosphere & Craft: Defend the poster's emotional atmosphere and production-level finish. Evaluate palette, tonal contrast, color relationships, texture, depth, lighting, graphical consistency, and fine execution. Color and surface treatment should reinforce the concept and hierarchy. Reject both sterile default styling and gratuitous effects that are not integrated into the visual system.
