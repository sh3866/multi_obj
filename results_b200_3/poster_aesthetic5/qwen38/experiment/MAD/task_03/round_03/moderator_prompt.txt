- Visual Concept & Imagery: Defend the strength of the poster's central visual idea and image direction. - Composition & Spatial Hierarchy: Defend the poster as a strong spatial composition. - Typography & Message Integration: Defend typography as an expressive visual material, not merely readable text. - Color, Atmosphere & Craft: Defend the poster's emotional atmosphere and production-level finish. Evaluate palette, tonal contrast, color relationships, texture, depth, lighting, graphical consistency, and fine execution. Color and surface treatment should reinforce the concept and hierarchy. Reject both sterile default styling and gratuitous effects that are not integrated into the visual system.

Create an atmospheric exhibition poster for a museum show about life in the deepest parts of the ocean. The imagery should evoke scientific curiosity and genuine mystery rather than looking like a generic aquarium advertisement. Do not design a museum webpage, scientific dashboard, collection of cards, or informational interface. Create one unified poster composition.
