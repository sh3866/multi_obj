PROTECT:
The clean, dark-mode aesthetic and the consistent typographic hierarchy (Name > Tagline > Price > Features) are strong foundations that provide a premium feel and ensure readability.
TRADE-OFF:
The revision prioritizes 'Focal Distinctiveness' and 'Comparison Utility' over 'Uniform Simplicity.' While the current design is clean, it is too uniform; the revision introduces visual variation (scale, illustration detail) to better serve the user's need to identify the best product and understand the differences between them.
