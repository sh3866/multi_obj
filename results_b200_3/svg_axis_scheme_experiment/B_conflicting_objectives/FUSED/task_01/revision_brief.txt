PRIORITIZED CHANGES:
1. **Enhance Headliner Dominance:** Expand the 'Luna Horizon' section vertically to break the grid rhythm, using a larger, more expressive typeface and a background glow to make it the undeniable focal point. 2. **Introduce Typographic Hierarchy:** Vary the weight or size of artist names based on their 'tier' (e.g., slightly larger for mid-tier acts) to create a visual pulse within the schedule. 3. **Refine Event Markers:** Replace the simple colored dots with more expressive icons or shapes that subtly reflect the genre (e.g., a wave for ambient, a bolt for punk) while maintaining the stage color palette. 4. **Improve Grid Breathing Room:** Increase the vertical padding between time slots to reduce the 'crowded' feel and allow the energetic colors to breathe.
PROTECT:
The clear, color-coded stage system (Yellow/Pink/Teal) is excellent for scanning and must be preserved. The overall dark mode aesthetic with the 'afterglow' gradient palette is a strong identity anchor.
TRADE-OFF:
The revision prioritizes 'Variety & Focal Distinctiveness' over strict 'Unity & System Consistency'. By breaking the grid for the headliner and varying the internal typography of the events, the design moves from a purely functional timetable to an expressive festival piece, accepting a slight increase in visual complexity to gain a stronger identity.
