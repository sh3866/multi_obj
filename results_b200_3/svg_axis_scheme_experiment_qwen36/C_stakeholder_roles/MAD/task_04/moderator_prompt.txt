Design a polished single-page product comparison for a fictional premium audio brand called "Auralith." Compare three wireless headphones: Auralith Air at $249 for lightweight everyday listening, Auralith Studio at $399 for accurate sound and creative work, and Auralith Nocturne at $549 for flagship immersive listening. The page must visibly include the brand name and positioning statement; a product visual for each headphone created with CSS or inline SVG; product name and price; three concise features per product; available color options; battery-life information; one clearly identified recommended model; and a purchase action for each product. All three products must use one disciplined comparison system so specifications, prices and differences can be compared quickly and fairly. At the same time, each product should have a meaningful visual personality connected to its intended use, and the recommended product must become the strongest focal point without making the other products appear unfinished or unimportant. Balance brand unity with distinct product identities, consistent comparison structure with meaningful visual variation, strong recommendation emphasis with fair comparison, and premium restraint with memorable visual richness. Do not differentiate products with unrelated card layouts, and do not achieve unity by making them visually interchangeable.
