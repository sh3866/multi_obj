PRIORITIZED CHANGES:
1. Restructure the layout into a 2-column asymmetric grid. The Feature Story should occupy the full width or a dominant 2/3 width at the top, establishing immediate hierarchy. 
2. Replace the vertical stack of secondary articles with a 2x2 grid. This allows for better comparison and visual rhythm, preventing the 'list' fatigue. 
3. Differentiate the secondary articles by category: use distinct typographic treatments (e.g., italics for Poetry, bold caps for Visual Art) rather than identical blocks, fulfilling the 'coherent but not identical' requirement. 
4. Integrate the 'distance' theme through negative space and overlapping elements (e.g., a large, faint typographic element spanning the background) rather than a literal grid texture. 
5. Elevate the 'Read the Issue' button to a fixed or sticky position, or place it prominently in the header, ensuring it is accessible without scrolling past the content.
PROTECT:
The typographic pairing (Serif for headlines, Sans-serif for metadata) is excellent and provides the necessary editorial tone. The color palette (muted beige, deep navy, rust red) is sophisticated and should be retained. The 'Horizon' graphic concept is strong but needs to be integrated more organically into the layout rather than sitting as a static box.
TRADE-OFF:
We are trading the 'cleanliness' of the current vertical list for the 'complexity' of a 2x2 grid. This increases cognitive load slightly but significantly improves the 'magazine' feel and allows for the requested visual variety among secondary stories without clutter.
