Morphology

Compound leaf, trifoliate. Leaflets obovate to orbicular, 20–40 mm long, with serrulate margins and characteristic pale basal blotch. Petiole 40–150 mm, terete, pubescent.

Serration count 8–12 / margin

Notes

Specimen collected from undisturbed meadow edge. Central leaflet exhibits pronounced white blotch — typical of T. repens. Marginal serrations fine and regular. No herbivory observed.
