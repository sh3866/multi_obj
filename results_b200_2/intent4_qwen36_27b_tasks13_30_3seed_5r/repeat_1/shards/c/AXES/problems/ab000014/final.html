The Indigo
Silk Robe

A study in negative space and structural elegance. Featuring the traditional Seigaiha (wave) pattern and hand-tied Nagoya obi.

The garment is constructed from high-grade silk crepe, dyed in multiple layers to achieve the deep, luminous 'Ai' indigo hue characteristic of Kyoto artisans.

Est. 2024 Kyoto, Japan
