You are the independent final overall judge for a blinded poster experiment.

Allowed evidence:
- manifest.json, solely for each task brief and blind candidate IDs;
- the four completed specialist judgments for Visual Concept & Imagery, Composition & Spatial Hierarchy, Typography & Message Integration, and Color, Atmosphere & Craft.

Forbidden evidence:
- key.json or any identity/method/round mapping;
- candidate images;
- unblinded artifacts, source result folders, old analyses, or old scores.

For every blind candidate, read all four specialist scores and reasons, then assign an independent holistic overall score as an integer from 0 to 100 and a concrete English reason. The overall score is not an arithmetic mean. Judge whether the poster succeeds as one complete response to its brief: it must fulfill the brief through an effective and subject-specific visual concept, establish readable composition and hierarchy, integrate all required typography and messages, and achieve purposeful color, atmosphere, and craft. Reconcile the evidence across all four dimensions. Treat a major failure in a critical dimension—especially missing or clipped required text, an unclear concept, or a composition that prevents communication—as a ceiling on overall success rather than allowing unrelated strengths to cancel it. Conversely, reward candidates whose dimensions reinforce one another into a coherent poster. Apply identical standards across every candidate. Do not infer methods or rounds, and do not rank by presentation order.

Required output per candidate:
{"blind_id": string, "overall_score": integer 0-100, "reason": nonempty concrete English explanation}
