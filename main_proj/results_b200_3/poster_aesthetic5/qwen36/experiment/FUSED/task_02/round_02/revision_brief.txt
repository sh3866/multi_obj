PRIORITIZED CHANGES:
Replace the rigid geometric vector figure with a high-contrast, expressive visual that captures the tension between control and release. Use a technique such as long-exposure photography, charcoal smudging, or a fragmented silhouette that suggests motion blur or disintegration. The figure should feel organic and heavy yet ethereal. Re-integrate the typography so that the title 'THE WEIGHT OF AIR' interacts with the figure—perhaps the text is being pushed by the body, or the body is emerging from the negative space of the letters. Shift the layout to be more dynamic, perhaps off-center, to create visual tension. The color palette should remain monochromatic but introduce texture (grain, paper, or light leaks) to avoid the sterile digital look.
PROTECT:
The monochromatic color palette (black and white/grey) is appropriate for the subject matter and should be retained. The bold, sans-serif typeface choice is functional and modern, though its application needs to be more expressive.
TRADE-OFF:
Prioritize the expressiveness and 'physicality' of the central image over the clean, tidy legibility of the current layout. The design must sacrifice some 'corporate polish' to gain the necessary emotional grit and artistic ambiguity required for a contemporary dance performance.
