Replace the geometric wireframe organism with a high-fidelity, atmospheric image of a bioluminescent creature (e.g., a siphonophore or anglerfish) that feels tangible and biological, utilizing volumetric lighting to create depth. Shift the typography to a more refined hierarchy: use a sharp, high-contrast serif for the headline to evoke institutional authority and mystery, or a custom sans-serif that feels 'submerged' rather than glowing. Integrate the text with the environment by allowing the deep-sea darkness to encroach on the edges of the poster, perhaps using a vignette or subtle particle effects (marine snow) to create a sense of scale and pressure. The layout should feel less like a balanced slide and more like a glimpse into a dark abyss.
PROTECT:
The color palette of deep navy and cyan is effective and should be retained. The vertical orientation of the visual element is a good compositional choice to suggest depth and descent.
TRADE-OFF:
Prioritize atmospheric immersion and biological realism over graphic minimalism. The design must sacrifice some 'cleanliness' to gain the necessary sense of mystery and scale.
