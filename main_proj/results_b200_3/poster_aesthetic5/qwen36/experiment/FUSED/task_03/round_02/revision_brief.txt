Replace the geometric hull graphic with a high-contrast, atmospheric image of a bioluminescent deep-sea organism (e.g., a siphonophore or anglerfish) that feels alien and organic. Integrate the headline 'BELOW THE LAST LIGHT' into the darkness, perhaps using a subtle glow or texture to make it feel submerged. Shift the layout to a central or full-bleed composition where the text floats within the 'water' rather than sitting on top of a black void. PROTECT:

TRADE-OFF:
