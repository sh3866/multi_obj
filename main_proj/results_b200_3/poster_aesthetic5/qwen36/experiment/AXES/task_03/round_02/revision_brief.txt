PROTECT: The atmospheric background gradient and the delicate, glowing vertical line art that establishes the deep-sea setting.

PROTECT: The geometric, wireframe aesthetic of the marine life forms is a strong stylistic choice that differentiates the design from generic photography; this structural style must be preserved while improving the lighting.
