PRIORITIZED CHANGES:
1. Redesign the central visual to be more specific and urgent: replace the orb with a realistic, glowing ember or a falling cigarette butt, and transform the row of flames into a more organic, creeping fire line that threatens the silhouette of the trees. 2. Remove the solid black footer; instead, integrate the subtext and campaign name directly into the dark foreground of the landscape, using the natural shadows of the terrain to provide contrast. 3. Enhance the atmosphere by adding subtle smoke textures and a more dramatic, directional light source from the fire, creating a stronger sense of depth and heat. 4. Refine the typography: increase the weight and size of the headline to make it a dominant compositional element, and adjust the tracking to feel more deliberate and impactful.
PROTECT:
The color palette is effective; the deep browns, oranges, and blacks create a cohesive, moody atmosphere that is appropriate for the subject matter. The general layout—headline at the top, visual in the center, details at the bottom—provides a clear and logical visual path.
TRADE-OFF:
The revision prioritizes visual realism and atmospheric depth over the current clean, minimalist vector style. This trade-off is necessary to move the design from a generic illustration to a 'memorable, content-specific concept' that feels emotionally immediate, even if it requires more complex rendering of smoke and fire.
