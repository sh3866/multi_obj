PRIORITIZED CHANGES:
Reimagine the central visual to depict a specific, careless action (e.g., a discarded cigarette or a campfire) that is actively igniting a larger, more chaotic and organic fire. Introduce a sense of scale by showing the fire climbing a mountain slope, using jagged, irregular shapes for the flames and smoke to convey danger. Increase the typographic hierarchy by making the headline larger and bolder, and consider a more condensed or impactful typeface to match the urgency. Position the human figure to be more clearly connected to the source of the fire, emphasizing the 'one spark' narrative.
PROTECT:
The dark, high-contrast color palette effectively creates a serious and urgent atmosphere. The clear separation between the headline, the central image, and the instructional text provides a logical visual path for the viewer.
TRADE-OFF:
Prioritize visual storytelling and emotional impact over the current clean but generic aesthetic. The design must move from a static illustration to a dynamic scene that visually demonstrates the cause-and-effect relationship between a single spark and a wildfire.
