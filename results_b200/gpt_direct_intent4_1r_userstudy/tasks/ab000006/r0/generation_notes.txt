Audit narrative (not a raw model runtime response)

The screen is composed as a vintage cartridge-game title card framed by a navy pixel border. A large stepped mountain and ski run fill the background while the centered title panel creates a clear entry hierarchy. Pixel-built trees, flags, snowbanks, skier, trail, and a small pursuing yeti make the slope read as an active game world rather than wallpaper. A bright coral START button, terse instruction line, and compact top HUD satisfy the requested UI without diluting the snowy scene.
