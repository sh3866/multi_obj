Audit narrative (not a raw model runtime response)

The composition uses a low, cinematic view of diagonal timber boards to make the floor materially legible. A large irregular translucent spill occupies the central foreground and includes edge highlights, ripples, droplets, and a reflected window motif. The mop crosses the spill diagonally, with layered cloth strands and wet highlights making the cleaning action unmistakable. Warm amber light, a small bucket silhouette, and restrained upper-left titling establish a coherent domestic mood without competing with the scene.
