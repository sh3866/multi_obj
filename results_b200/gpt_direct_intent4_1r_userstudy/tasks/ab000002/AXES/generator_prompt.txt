Connector strokes meet node rims somewhat awkwardly and vary in brightness, making the graph feel less precisely constructed than the surrounding panels. The large empty lower portions of both sidebars make their panel rhythm feel unfinished.

SUGGESTION:

Rework the portrait into the same crisp vector/stroke language, align sidebar section baselines to the central panel, and add restrained dividers or structured lower modules so the side columns feel deliberately completed rather than merely empty.
