Audit narrative (not a raw model runtime response)

The interface treats the screen as an illustrated golden-hour meadow rather than a generic dashboard. Layered hills, reeds, flowers, clouds, and drifting motes give the play field depth. Four distinct floating creatures use different silhouettes and motion cues; the player and oversized net occupy the lower-right foreground. A compact glass HUD carries the title and collected count, while the collection button is prominent but subordinate to the living scene.
