Audit narrative (not a raw model runtime response)

The design uses a split but integrated arcade composition: a large chromatic pixel title and start control anchor the left, while the four-lane note highway dominates the right in steep perspective. Cyan, magenta, violet, and amber notes create lane-specific rhythm over a dark grid horizon. Scanlines, a sunset disc, waveform ornament, score preview, and precise neon framing reinforce a committed synthwave identity while keeping interaction hierarchy readable.
